TEF — Smart Practice Order

Prompts regrouped by theme and sequenced easiest → hardest, for compréhension écrite (ads) and expression écrite (Sections A & B).

Every list has a Sort by control at the top: keep the practice order, switch to difficulty order or coverage order, or — on the ad lists — regroup by any of ten clustering methods.

Coverage order — start here if you are short on time. Every item is ranked by how much of the rest of its list you can answer once you own that one, weighted by what the TEF rubrics actually score. Each item carries the reason it sits where it sits, two running meters — how much of the pool you now cover, and how much of the language machinery you have installed — and a banner at every point where stopping would still leave you somewhere worth being. The front of the list is worth many times the back; the last stretch is near-duplicates you can skip without losing anything.
